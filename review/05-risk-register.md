# Risk register

Revision 0.5 — 7 November 2026 — estimates for planning, no prototype evidence; microphonics, fan cooling and G2-07 preferred internal bank and G2-09 concurrent comparison tracked.

Consequence **C**: 1 minor inconvenience, 3 invalid results or repair, 5 potentially fatal shock/fire or major uncontrolled energy. Uncertainty **U**: 1 well evidenced, 3 incomplete analysis, 5 central behavior unspecified/unproved. Prototype effort **E**: 1 simple bounded bench check, 3 dedicated fixture/multi-range characterization, 5 custom isolated/high-energy fixture plus extensive fault tests. Scores are ordinal judgments, **not failure probabilities**. Priority ranks consequence/uncertainty first, then dependency; effort is shown separately, not multiplied into an artificial risk number.

K01–K03 remain high-consequence even after numerical limits are chosen. Closure requires evidence that protective measures bound faults, not just that nominal regulation works. K04 can also contribute to safety if precision sensing is reused as protection; that dependency is tracked under K05 and must not be hidden by its performance-focused consequence score.

Recommended experiment order is limited-energy safety/measurement infrastructure, heater/matrix coupons, then high-voltage extension. Priority rank does not authorize starting the most energetic test first. No experiments are authorized by a draft pass criterion alone; G1–G4 review and a concrete safe test setup precede energized work.

G2-09 makes simultaneous comparison the preferred mode and retains sequential operation. Extend the existing power/thermal, precision and matrix risks to omitted active loads, asymmetric channel demand, common-reference or supply coupling, cross-channel backfeed, incorrect result/fault attribution and timing skew. D002/D004/D010/D012/D018 own the unresolved limits; P-H/P-E/P-M/P-X require combined-path evidence in addition to individual-path tests. The earlier sequential-only recommendation is superseded. Risk scores are unchanged pending architecture evidence; concurrent operation is not assumed to double the approved power budget.

## Review contract

G1-06 adds a microphonics requirement to the existing risk work: K04 measurement accuracy must include stimulus/response repeatability and fixture/background artifacts; K05 access/safety must include the selected fixture and any powered stimulus faults; K08 scope growth must account for its interface, power and integration needs. D022 and prototype P-T own this added coverage. Numerical stimulus limits and any change to risk scores require method selection and review; inclusion alone does not establish a safe or reproducible implementation.

G2-06 agrees preferred fan-assisted cooling (R082). K04 now explicitly includes fan-drive electrical interference and fan/airflow vibration in audio and microphonics measurements across approved fan states. K06 includes the continuous 0–40 °C ambient requirement (R081), fan power/heat, blocked airflow, loss of required cooling, and controller/temperature-sensor faults. I020 and P-E/P-M/P-T carry these checks. Noise/fault thresholds, hardware and any fan-off operating region remain Open; scores, ranking and effort estimates are unchanged.

G2-07 prefers the bank internally, with placement Provisional. K06 must evaluate bank dissipation plus conversion/auxiliary heat in the shared enclosure at 40 °C, including hot spots, airflow distribution, loss of required cooling and retained hot/charged parts. K04 must evaluate the resulting analog/reference drift and fan/noise interaction. R037/R038/R068 and I019/I020 retain thermal and protective separation; fan availability alone is not closure evidence. Scores remain unchanged.
